Pasta, bacon and eggs



This is a recipe of how to make pasta, bacon and eggs! (Large portions!)

Ingredients:

  • 1 pack of bacon (180-200g each) per person
  • 2-4 eggs per person
  • 200-250g pasta per person
  • oil or butter for frying

Cooking steps:

  1. Slice the bacon into desired shape and size, 2cm wide strips for example.
  2. Fry the bacon to desired crispiness, as you start frying the bacon, start bringing pasta water to a boil.
  3. Put pasta into the pot of boiling water while as soon as you can and cook the pasta according to instructions.
  4. Put fried bacon to the side, for example in the bowl you're going to eat from and cover with aluminium foil to keep warm.
  5. Start frying eggs, if your pasta is done before the eggs, start draining the pasta!
  6. Assemble the bowl of food and enjoy! Tip for ketchup lovers... go for it!
